SciPy and NumPy are optional separately installed dependencies. Their source
code, binaries and licenses are not bundled here, and this package's MIT
license does not relicense those dependencies. SciPy and its contributors
provide the LAPJVsp candidate solver used by the optional path.

The sparse doubling construction and min-cost-flow cut/potential optimality
conditions are established methods; no algorithmic novelty is claimed.
Credit for the standard theory and relevant implementations is given in
methods_results.txt and references.json, including SciPy's documentation and
the Kiraly–Kovacs paper describing LEMON implementations. No SciPy or LEMON
implementation is copied into this package.
